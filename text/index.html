WELCOME!

================================================================================

The mission of the International Radio Network is to promote the amateur radio hobby to those interested in radio communications. We welcome licensed and unlicensed operators, giving those with an interest the opportunity to gain experience in radio communications, theory and technology, build fellowship, and have some fun along the way.
