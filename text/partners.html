WHO ARE THE IRN’S PARTNERS AND FRIENDS?

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  • ZELLO MODE RADIO (ZMR) - The IRN also enjoys the partnership with the Zello Mode Radio group, also known as ZMR. They have a Zello channel connected to our multimode system open to licensed operators, and another connected to the ‘non-RF’ channels, which are open to both licensed and unlicensed members. You can learn more about the ZMR group by visiting their website, https://www.zmr.us.

The IRN has close links with several other Networks and Groups around the world, and we constantly seek to engage with others and link up to some of the best Nets throughout the week for the enjoyment of our members.
