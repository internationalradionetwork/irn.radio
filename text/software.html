WHAT ABOUT SOFTWARE?

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

To start, we recommend that you try either Zello or TeamSpeak 3.

Zello

Zello is a great walkie talkie app that works well on most platforms, and is free for personal use. It is available for the desktop, for Android devices, and for iOS. Please follow this link to select the correct download, based on your operating system.

TeamSpeak 3

TeamSpeak 3 is another great app that runs on most platforms, is easy to set up, and provides excellent audio quality. Please follow this link to select the correct download, based on your operating system.

Either app can be downloaded from Google Play, from Apple, or directly from their sites.

Channels are crosslinked between Zello and TeamSpeak, so there is no need to have both applications, but many members choose to do so.
