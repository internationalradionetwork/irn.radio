Nets on the IRN

Nets are at the heart of IRN operations. We hold them throughout the week, led by net controllers from North America, Europe, the Caribbean and elsewhere, so every net benefits from a different background and perspective.

All of our nets are open to licensed operators, with some open for all to enjoy. Please stop by to say hello. We look forward to hearing from you!

Nets open to licensed operators

  • KB1 Multimode Net: every Friday, 4:00 pm Eastern / 9:00 pm UK

Nets open to all

  • Friendship Net: every Monday, 5:00 pm Eastern / 10:00 pm UK
  • Coffee Net: every Wednesday, 5:00 pm Eastern / 10:00 pm UK

This schedule lists only the nets run by the IRN on its own channels. You may also hear other nets on the IRN multimode connections. Throughout the week we link to selected nets from partner networks that we think our members will enjoy. We review these links regularly and add new ones from time to time.

For full connection details for licensed and unlicensed users, see our connections page.
